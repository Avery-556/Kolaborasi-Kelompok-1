Adelia Fatawy The Second Life of a Forgotten Machine

In this digital era, electronic waste is not difficult to find. Unusable and discarded items can lead to excessive waste accumulation. However, if reused, such as a CPU, which contains a reusable fan and module, it can become a more useful item.
A CPU can be disassembled and its components separated, separating the cables, the module, and the fan inside. From there, both materials can be reused to form the main frame of a new product.
These two materials can then be combined with new, functional cables. With proper arrangement and a little creativity, these components can become a more useful item.
This creates a fan that can be used to provide sufficient airflow in hot weather.

Hanifah Nursyifa Chasing the Sun, Beneath the Breeze

In this modern era, awareness regarding the use of renewable energy is growing, one example being the application of small-scale solar panels. Items often considered trivial or overlooked can be transformed into useful innovations. A concrete example of this is the integration of a mini solar panel, a small motor, and an ordinary hat to create headwear that is both functional and cooling.
The creation process begins by preparing a plain hat and the necessary electronic components, including a mini solar panel, thin connecting wires, and a small, low-power fan. The solar panel is positioned on the brim of the hat to capture maximum sunlight, while the fan is mounted at an optimal angle to direct airflow toward the wearer's face.
Next, the components are assembled by connecting the wires from the solar panel directly to the fan motor. With careful attention to tidying up the wiring and ensuring the solar panel is securely attached to the fabric of the hat, this simple system is ready to operate automatically whenever it is exposed to sunlight—no additional batteries required.
This assembly process results in an innovative, solar-cooled hat that is highly practical for outdoor activities, especially during hot, sweltering weather.

Rava Anand Crisha Don't Throw It Away, Wear It!

In today’s modern era, electronic waste has become a common sight. If left to accumulate, these obsolete items can easily lead to environmental pollution. However, if the components within them are managed wisely—such as by repurposing fans and modules from discarded computer equipment—this waste can be transformed into something far more functional.
The repurposing process begins by carefully dismantling old computer units to extract key parts, ranging from wiring and control modules to the internal fan blades. These salvaged components are then prepared to be reassembled into the foundational structure of a new innovation.
These still-usable, salvaged components are combined with new, high-quality wiring and a used hat. Through meticulous assembly and a touch of creativity, these separate parts are integrated into a functional, reusable device.
The final result is a wearable, mini-fan hat—a practical tool that helps provide a cooling breeze during hot weather.
